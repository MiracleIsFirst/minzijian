Research agenda

Reliable learning for visual restoration

Robust Image Restoration

Robust estimation, blind deblurring, outlier suppression, and restoration under imperfect degradation models.

Generative Restoration

Diffusion and flow-based priors for inverse problems, with emphasis on structural fidelity and robustness.

Learning to Optimize

Reinforcement learning and test-time optimization for adapting generative restoration models to perceptual objectives.
